# Password Reset Policy

## Overview
This document outlines the password reset procedures for all users.

## Web Application Reset

### Step 1: Navigate to Login Page
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password?"

### Step 2: Enter Email
Enter your registered email address and click "Send Reset Link"

### Step 3: Check Email
Check your email inbox for a password reset link. The link expires in 24 hours.

### Step 4: Create New Password
Click the link and enter your new password. Password must be:
- At least 12 characters long
- Include uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include at least one number
- Include at least one special character

## Mobile App Reset

### iOS Instructions
1. Open the app
2. Tap "Settings" > "Account" > "Reset Password"
3. Follow the email verification process

### Android Instructions
1. Open the app
2. Navigate to Menu > Account Settings > Security
3. Tap "Change Password"
4. Follow the email verification process

## Security Notes
- Never share your password reset link
- Reset links expire after 24 hours
- Contact support if you don't receive the email within 5 minutes
- Use a unique password that you haven't used before

## Troubleshooting

### Email Not Received
- Check spam/junk folder
- Verify email address is correct
- Wait 5 minutes before requesting another reset
- Contact support@example.com if issue persists

### Link Expired
- Request a new password reset link
- Complete the process within 24 hours

### Password Requirements Not Met
Ensure your password includes:
- Minimum 12 characters
- Mix of upper and lowercase
- At least one number (0-9)
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
